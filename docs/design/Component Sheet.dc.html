2 · Ball states (5 colours × normal / highlighted / focused / bursting)
The ball layer is identical in every state. Highlighting paints the cell behind it (#7486C4, 3.2:1 against white), focus draws a black ring with a white keyline outside it, and bursting scales the ball while the cell colour stays behind it.

3 · Greyscale Breakers and optional shape hints
Each grey uses the same light position and edge fall-off as the colour balls. They differ in both lightness and pattern, and the pattern alone is enough to tell them apart. Shape hints are an opt-in setting for the Colorful set and are off by default, so idle balls match the reference.
