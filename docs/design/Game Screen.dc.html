How to Play
Tap a group of two or more touching balls of the same color. The group is highlighted and shows the points it is worth.
Tap the group again to burst it. Balls above fall down, and empty columns close up to the right.
Points = n × (n − 1)

Big groups score far more, so plan ahead. The game ends when no two touching balls share a color. There is no undo.
Keyboard: arrow keys move, Enter or Space selects and bursts, Esc clears the selection.
